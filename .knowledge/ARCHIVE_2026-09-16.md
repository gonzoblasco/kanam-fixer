   - The `assert_announcement` tool matches the *exact ordered* sequence of phrases; a subset triggers `passed:false` – an important contract that must be documented for downstream users.
   - Using the client library (`@modelcontextprotocol/sdk`) provides a realistic end‑to‑end test, more reliable than in‑memory transport alone.
2. **Publishing mechanics**

   - License must be present both in `package.json` (`"license":"MIT"`) and as a `LICENSE` file; npm automatically includes it in the tarball.
